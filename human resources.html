HR Vocabulary

Vocabulary with Examples



  • Onboard = to integrate and train a new employee into an organization

    Example: It takes about two weeks to fully onboard new team members.

  • Headhunt = to actively recruit high-performing individuals from other companies

    Example: The executive was headhunted by a competing firm.

  • Lay off = to terminate employment due to operational or financial reasons

    Example: The factory had to lay off fifty workers during the restructuring.

  • Stand down = to temporarily step aside or leave a position

    Example: The manager decided to stand down while the investigation took place.

  • Up-skill = to teach employees additional or advanced skills

    Example: We offer regular training courses to help staff up-skill.

  • Fringe benefit = an additional compensation provided beyond a regular salary

    Example: Health insurance and company cars are common fringe benefits.

  • Severance pay = compensation paid to an employee upon involuntary termination

    Example: He received three months of severance pay after the redundancy.

  • Talent acquisition = the process of finding and hiring skilled workers

    Example: Our talent acquisition team is focused on filling technical roles.

  • Performance review = a formal evaluation of an employee's work and progress

    Example: Annual performance reviews take place every December.

  • Exit interview = a meeting with a departing employee to gather feedback

    Example: During her exit interview, she suggested ways to improve workplace culture.

  • Probation period = a trial period for new hires to assess their performance

    Example: New employees must complete a three-month probation period.

  • Grievance procedure = an official process for handling employee complaints

    Example: The HR manual outlines the formal grievance procedure step-by-step.
