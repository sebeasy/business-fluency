Marketing

Vocabulary with Examples



  • Target market = the specific group of customers a company aims to sell to

    Example: Our target market is young professionals aged 25 to 35.

  • Brand awareness = the level of recognition customers have of a brand

    Example: The new campaign significantly increased our brand awareness.

  • Market research = the process of gathering information about customers and competitors

    Example: We conducted market research before launching the new product.

  • Campaign = a planned set of activities to promote a product or service

    Example: The summer marketing campaign was very successful.

  • USP (Unique Selling Point) = the special feature that makes a product different from others

    Example: Our USP is the eco-friendly packaging.

  • Positioning = the way a brand is presented in the minds of consumers

    Example: The company is positioning itself as a premium luxury brand.

  • Segmentation = dividing the market into different groups of customers

    Example: Demographic segmentation helps us understand our audience better.

  • ROI (Return on Investment) = a measure of the profit gained from an investment

    Example: We achieved a very high ROI on our social media advertising.

  • Lead generation = the process of attracting potential customers

    Example: The website redesign helped improve lead generation.

  • Conversion rate = the percentage of visitors who complete a desired action

    Example: Our conversion rate increased from 2% to 5% after the changes.

  • SEO (Search Engine Optimization) = the practice of improving a website’s visibility on search engines

    Example: We hired an expert to improve our SEO ranking.

  • Content marketing = creating and sharing valuable content to attract and retain customers

    Example: Content marketing has become central to our strategy.

  • Influencer marketing = promoting products through influential people on social media

    Example: The influencer marketing campaign reached over one million people.

  • CTA (Call to Action) = a prompt that encourages the audience to take a specific action

    Example: The “Buy Now” button is our strongest CTA.

  • Market share = the percentage of total sales a company has in a particular market

    Example: We want to increase our market share in Latin America.

  • Branding = the process of creating a unique image and identity for a product or company

    Example: Strong branding helps build customer loyalty.
